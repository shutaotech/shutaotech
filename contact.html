Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common inquiries about our products and services.

How can I download your mobile applications?
You can download our mobile applications from Google Play Store (for Android devices) or App Store (for iOS devices). Simply search "Shutao" or visit our developer pages directly.
Do you offer custom development services?
Yes, we provide custom development services for electronic equipment, mobile applications, and smart home solutions. Please contact our business development team for more information.
What areas do you ship your products to?
We ship our products globally. Our electronic components and smart home devices are available for international delivery. Contact us for specific shipping information to your region.
How can I become a business partner?
We welcome business partnerships and collaborations. Please reach out to our business development team at sunliyuan@shutaotech.com with your partnership proposal.
What kind of technical support do you offer?
We offer comprehensive technical support for all our products and services. You can reach our support team via email at support@shutaotech.com or through our mobile applications.
Are your smart home products compatible with other brands?
Our smart home solutions are designed to work with various industry-standard protocols and can integrate with many popular smart home ecosystems. Contact us for specific compatibility information.
